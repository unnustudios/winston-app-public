Winston

A quiet place for the things you meant to do — focus periods that actually block your phone, the books you are working through, and the habits worth keeping.

What it does

  • Focus periods — set aside part of the day and Winston shields every app except the ones you allow, using Apple's Screen Time system.
  • Books and courses — keep the things you are working through in one place, with your progress alongside them.
  • Habits and schedule — small daily habits and weekly sessions, laid out over your day so you can see whether the plan and the day agree.

Quiet by design

No ads, no analytics about you, no trackers, and nothing to sell. Winston works fully without an account, and which apps you block never leaves your device. The privacy policy sets out the details.
